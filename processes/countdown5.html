on faith

there is a specific kind of exhaustion that comes from holding things together with nothing but repetition. these are the entries written when the machinery stopped humming, left out in the open where the weather could get to them.

nothing here is sacred on purpose. it’s just what remained when the rooms emptied out and the light changed color, forcing us to look at the convents we built our grief inside of.

If there is any grace to be found here, it's accidental—wrenched out of exhaustion rather than hope. Take what you need from the rooms we left behind.
