author's note

There are people in our lives who come and go like the tides. The thing is, tides will never stop, and will never care about the rocks they weather, the sands they wash away, or the moon that dictates their whole existence.

People—friendships and the sense of self—are like waves that keep coming back. Like how no wave is identical to another, some people are meant to be remembered, some remembered only to be forgotten, and some we can no longer recall at all.

But in itself, tides are a cycle: death and revitalization. We crash and burn to be molded again by forces beyond this world—yet there is beauty in not knowing where the tides will take us.
