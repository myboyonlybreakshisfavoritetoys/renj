junction

Lines of white cover the junctions of wonder,
Those who cross paths once with the others,
Gazes that only exist less than a minute,
Limited by the rests of the vehicles beside it.

But some gazes wonder more than others,
A red dress brushing my brown leather,
Identifying the dues of the time between rests,
A wonder most of humanity come to obsess.

Vulnerable eyes cross the astute windows,
That show how I’ve wept like widows.
Lands beyond the concrete of the road,
Idealized by thoughts I overload.

Tomorrow, I’ll be down this street again,
With the pollute of the mind, dirtiest of chagrin,
I’d stare by the countdown to start the pause,
To try and fill the blank after my “because.”

For a mere thirty seconds, a soul is removed,
A cotton hem when the pause is on the move.
What could it be? A fleet of sighs, perhaps?
Or a happenstance I want to be a routine afast?
